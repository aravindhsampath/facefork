Every share format in the new Share this creation dialog, rendered live from the Mona Lisa demo tree — loading…. Each plate below is drawn by the same src/share code the app ships, so what you see is what a user would post. Mark what to keep, cut the rest, and send me the list.

Provenance

The painting is Leonardo's, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons. The six “edits” hanging off it were drawn here in your browser with canvas — a real bezier mustache, real drawn aviators, real filters — not generated by an image model, because that would spend your OpenRouter credit to build a demo. Duchamp drew his mustache by hand in 1919, so the stand-ins are at least in period.

In the app these are model output. Nothing else about these plates is faked: the layouts, the costs on the receipt, the tree geometry and the animation timings are all real.
